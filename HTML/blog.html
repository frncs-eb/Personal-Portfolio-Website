Hi Welcome!

In this section you will know the story of how my team and I created this system.

This project was made for our web development subject. Our objectives in this subject were to create a private portfolio for users who want to have their own portfolio to gather projects they've done but aren't ready to share publicly yet. We started by brainstorming ideas and decided to create a system that allows users to register, login, and manage their portfolios. We divided the tasks among ourselves, with each member focusing on different aspects of the project.
